1. Who we are

FinPulse (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal finance and productivity app for Android published by Narayana Parsha (“the developer”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. By using the app you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. What we do and do not collect

We do not collect personal data. Specifically, the developer does not:

  • operate a backend, database or analytics service for the app;
  • receive your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, financial records, notes, tasks or any other content you enter;
  • use advertising or tracking SDKs of our own, or sell, rent or trade any data;
  • access your Google Drive files, other than the single hidden backup file described in section 4.

Some information is processed on your device or by Google as described below. That processing happens between you and Google, or entirely on your phone — not through us.

3. Data stored on your device

FinPulse is offline-first. The content you create is stored locally in the app’s private storage:

4. Google sign-in and Drive backup

Signing in is optional and is used only to back up and restore your data.

  • Google Sign-In provides the app with your Google account name and e-mail address. These are kept only on your device to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive access uses the restricted drive.appdata scope. This gives FinPulse access only to a hidden “App Data” folder created for the app inside your Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files, and other apps cannot read FinPulse’s folder.
  • The backup is a single JSON file containing your records and basic settings (currency, number format, display name). Your PIN is never included.
  • Data travels directly between your device and Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. It does not pass through any server of ours.
  • The backup is stored under your Google account and is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and your Drive settings.
Google API Services User Data Policy. FinPulse’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup/restore feature the user sees in the app. It is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

5. Security and lock screen

We design FinPulse to keep your financial information private:

  • App lock: choose a 4-digit PIN or biometric unlock (fingerprint / device credential via the Android system prompt). You can lock the whole app, or only Money Book, Notes and Spend.
  • PIN protection: your PIN is stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash, never in clear text, and is excluded from backups. FinPulse never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data; biometric checks are handled entirely by Android.
  • Private storage: data is kept in the app’s private area, Android’s own cloud auto-backup is turned off for the app, and internal components (reminder receivers) are not exported to other apps.
  • Least access: the app asks only for permissions it needs (section 7) and uses the narrowest Google Drive scope available.
  • Secure transfer: all communication with Google services uses HTTPS.
Please change the default PIN. Until you set your own PIN, the app uses a default. Set a unique PIN in Settings, keep your phone’s screen lock enabled, and keep your device and Google account protected (strong password and 2-step verification). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you forget your PIN or lose access to your device, we cannot recover it for you.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FinPulse is free and shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google’s SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, such as:

  • advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and similar device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes, performance).

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller/processor under its own terms; the developer does not receive your personal data and only sees aggregated reports (for example, ad revenue and impressions). Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob & Firebase data disclosure.

Your ad choices and consent

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app displays a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and you can accept, decline or change your choices.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation at Settings → Privacy → Ads (path may vary by device).
  • Ads never access the content of your Money Book, notes, tasks or expenses.

8. Sharing and reports

Reports (PDF or image) are generated on your device. They are shared only when you choose to share them, using Android’s share sheet, with the app you pick. After you share, the receiving app’s own privacy practices apply. We do not sell or disclose your data to third parties, and we would disclose information only if required by applicable law (which is unlikely since we do not hold your data).

9. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • In Google Drive: the backup stays in your Drive’s hidden app data until you delete it. Uninstalling the app does not remove it, so that you can restore later.
  • With the developer: nothing — we hold no copy of your data.

Step-by-step instructions are on our Data Deletion page.

10. Your rights (worldwide)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including to access, correct, delete, export, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. These rights are recognised, for example, under the EU/UK GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA, Brazil’s LGPD, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Canada’s PIPEDA, Australia’s Privacy Act and similar laws.

Because FinPulse keeps your data on your device and in your own Google account, you already control it directly: view and edit it in the app, export it as reports, withdraw Google access at any time, and delete it as described in section 9. For ad-related data held by Google, you can use Google’s privacy tools at myaccount.google.com and the ad choices in section 6.

  • We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.
  • Legal bases (GDPR): where Google processes data for ads we rely on your consent (UMP); the optional Drive backup runs on your request and consent when you sign in.

If you have any privacy request or question, contact us (section 14) and we will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the time required by law.

11. Children’s privacy

FinPulse is a general-audience finance tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help you resolve it. Parents and guardians can use Android’s Family Link and Google Play controls to manage ads and app installs.

12. International users

The app can be used worldwide. Because the developer does not collect your data, there is no transfer of your personal data to us. Data you back up to Google Drive, and data processed by Google AdMob, is handled by Google in the locations and under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy documentation.
