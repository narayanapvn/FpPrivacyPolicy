Data Deletion

FinPulse does not keep your data on any server of ours, so you are in full control and can remove everything yourself. There is nothing for us to delete, but here is how to erase it all.

1. Delete data on your phone

  • Single items: delete records, expenses, tasks or notes inside the app.
  • Everything: Android Settings → Apps → FinPulse → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

2. Delete your Google Drive backup

The backup is a hidden file in your Drive’s App Data folder. To remove it:

  1. Open drive.google.com on a computer and go to Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps.
  2. Find FinPulse and choose Options → Delete hidden app data.

Menu names may change slightly over time. Deleting hidden app data permanently removes your cloud backup.

3. Remove FinPulse’s access to your Google account

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select FinPulse and choose Delete all connections (or “Remove access”).

4. Ad data

To reset or limit advertising data, go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads and choose Delete advertising ID / opt out of personalisation, and review Google My Ad Center.
