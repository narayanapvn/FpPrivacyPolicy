FinPulse

Your private, offline-first money companion: interest & EMI calculators, Money Book, daily spending, to-dos and notes — protected by a lock screen and backed up to your own Google Drive.

Privacy PolicyTerms & Conditions

🔒 Locked & secure

Protect the app with a PIN or your fingerprint / device credential. Lock the whole app or just Money Book, Notes and Spend.

👤 We collect no personal data

FinPulse has no developer server and no account with us. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer.

☁️ Your own Google Drive

Optional backup is saved in a hidden, app-only folder in your Google Drive — so you can restore on a new phone.

What you can do with FinPulse

  • Calculators – simple interest, compound interest, EMI and age.
  • Money Book – track money you lend or borrow, with interest and status, and share PDF/image reports.
  • Daily Spend – log expenses and view history and summaries.
  • To-Do & Notes – tasks with repeat and reminders, plus plain notes.
